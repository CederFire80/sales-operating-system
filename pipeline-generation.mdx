Pipeline a team creates for itself is the pipeline it can count on. I build that with one standing weekly session where the whole team prospects together, led from the front, with enough ritual and energy that people want to be in the room. This is the single mechanism I reach for first.

## Why it matters

Pipeline a rep sourced is pipeline a rep understands, forecasts, and can expand. Building it is a learnable motion, and the fastest way to teach it is to do it together, out loud, every week, with the leader on the phones alongside the team. The session makes prospecting the hour that sets the tempo for the week.

<Note>
  Your destiny is your pipeline. Generate it together, and the whole week organizes around the hour you spend creating it.
</Note>

## How I run it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make it a standing weekly block the whole team runs together">
    One recurring session, same time every week, treated as the most important hour on the calendar. In the first weeks I run it as a listening and best-practice exchange, so the team teaches me what already works before I ask anyone to change anything. Then it becomes the engine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Come prepared, so the hour is all execution">
    Each rep brings a focused, prepared target set to the session, built ahead of time, so the block itself is spent reaching people. The plan goes in before the session; the session is for the work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dial together, and run the first calls yourself">
    The team prospects in the same room, or on a shared floor when remote, so the good lines travel and the energy is shared. The leader makes the first calls, which sets the standard the team rises to and earns the right to coach what comes next.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wire in the rest of the funnel, so the hour compounds">
    Bring the SDRs into the same targets and the marketing team into account-based plays around them, so a rep's outbound, the SDR's support, and the air cover all land on the same accounts in the same week. The session becomes the front end of a coordinated engine.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## One version that worked for me

<Tip>
  It began with a gong and a couple of traffic cones. I set the cones by a conference room, put a big gong inside, and made the first cold call myself. I booked a meeting and hit the gong, and people from across the office came over to see what the noise was. An hour in, the reps wanted to run it again, and people who had never made a cold call asked to stay longer.

  The principle is the part that travels: energy is something you build on purpose, and the form it takes is up to you and your team. The gong worked for me, and yours will look like something else. Find the thing that makes booking a meeting feel like a win worth sharing, and the lift shows up the next week.
</Tip>

## What good looks like

- Self-sourced meetings per rep climb from the first few weeks of running the session.
- Reps who had never prospected are booking meetings, and asking to stay in the room longer.
- Pipeline the team created for itself grows as a share of the total.
- The session runs on its own energy, and the week takes its tempo from it.
