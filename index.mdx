This is the system I use to build a sales team that generates its own pipeline, runs on a steady weekly rhythm, and measures every gain. Each page is one working part, grounded in what I have actually run. Read them in order, or jump to the part you need.

<Note>
  Manage the inputs, and the output takes care of itself. The goal is the median rep performing like the top rep, on a motion that creates its own pipeline.
</Note>
